My doctoral research, conducted in the Sheffield Zooarchaeology Lab at the University of Sheffield, examines the origins of animal traction in Britain, focusing on the osteological evidence for the draught use of cattle. This project emphasises paleopathology and the morphological changes associated with animal management. More broadly, my research interests include human–animal relationships in the past, particularly within British prehistory, biometry, and domestication. I also have extensive hands-on experience preparing mammal and bird skeletons. When I’m not buried in bones, I’m either on the tennis court or in the swimming pool.
